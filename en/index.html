An iPhone app for video creators

You shoot. Your crew does the rest.

Shoot on location, edit by talking to Miguelón and his crew, cut clips from your live streams and publish to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. All from your iPhone, and your video is never stored on a server.

Scene 1 · Paris, 1902

Once upon a time, a magician made films all by himself.

Georges Méliès came from stage magic. He built a glass studio outside Paris, and there he wrote, directed, acted, painted the sets and invented the effects of hundreds of films. He had no crew: he was the crew.

  1. Producer
  2. Writer
  3. Director
  4. Actor
  5. Set designer
  6. Effects wizard

In 1902 he fired a rocket into the Moon’s eye: A Trip to the Moon. A hundred and twenty-four years later, the one doing everything is you.

Scene 2 · Today

You do everything too.

You shoot on location and remember the missing shot once you’re home. You cut the silences, add word-by-word subtitles, hunt for a hook for the first seconds, scrub through three hours of live stream to find one good minute, and publish to four networks, each with its own rules.

Scene 3 · The cast

You talk to one. Five get to work.

A crew of agents lives inside Melies. You only talk to Miguelón, by voice or in writing, the way you’d talk to your editor. He hands out the work, and every answer tells you who handled it. (The app speaks Spanish.)

  • Miguelón

    The one who listens

    Ask him for anything and he passes it to the right agent. When a request spans several areas, he handles it himself.

  • Abigail

    Edits and animates

    Text, subtitles, animations, arrows, zoom, music and the finishing touches.

  • El Güero

    Cuts clips

    Finds the best moments of your live streams and long videos, and takes care of pacing and cuts.

  • Memo

    Publishes and schedules

    Your posts on every network, your calendar and your scheduled live streams.

  • Kuki

    Ideas and hooks

    Hook ideas, scripts and what to shoot next time.

  1. 1 · On location

    While you shoot on site

    A checklist of what to shoot (the hook, the context, the place, what you lived there, the goodbye) so you don’t forget a thing. And if you’re live, your panel: viewers, marked moments, and your Kick title.

  2. 2 · Edit by talking

    Say what you want and watch it happen

    “Remove the silences”, “yellow subtitles”, “an arrow to the menu at second 7”. A real timeline with cuts, text, animations and music, vertical 9:16 or horizontal 16:9 for YouTube.

  3. 3 · Clips from long videos

    Three hours of stream, three good minutes

    From a live stream or a long video, Melies finds the best moments, by what you say and what’s on screen, and leaves them as vertical clips for you to review. It can also build a YouTube highlights video.

  4. 4 · Publish and schedule

    All your networks, whenever you choose

    Pick the accounts, write the caption, set the date and time and review what each network will show. Melies posts it to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Nothing goes out without your approval.

Scene 5 · Behind the scenes

Your video lives on your phone.

Melies edits and renders your video on your iPhone, with Apple’s own frameworks. Our server never stores your videos.

  1. Edited and rendered on your iPhone

    Your phone produces the final MP4 and saves it to your camera roll. Subtitles come from Apple’s speech recognition, on the same iPhone.

  2. The crew reads text, not your video

    To edit, Miguelón gets a text description of your timeline. To find moments, a few small still frames that are deleted once answered.

  3. Only what you publish travels, then it’s deleted

    When you schedule a post, the file waits only until it reaches your networks and is deleted as soon as they confirm: 48 hours at most.
