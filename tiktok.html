Melies and TikTok

How the Melies iPhone app connects your TikTok account and posts a video for you.

Melies is an iPhone app for video creators (iOS 17 or later). Today it is distributed through Apple's TestFlight while we prepare the App Store release; to get access, write to contacto@meliesapp.com. This website, meliesapp.com, is the official site of the app: it hosts our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the TikTok sign-in returns to the app through our server. The app's interface is in Spanish.

1. Connect your TikTok account (Login Kit)

In the app: Más › Configuración › Redes sociales › TikTok › Conectar (More › Settings › Social networks › TikTok › Connect). TikTok's own authorization page opens with the name Melies and the permissions we request:

  • user.info.basic — your open ID, display name and avatar, only to show which account is connected and where a video will be posted.
  • video.publish — to post a video you approved, with the settings you chose, to your profile.

You can disconnect at any time in the same screen, or remove Melies in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions).

2. The “Post to TikTok” screen

When you schedule or post a video, Melies asks TikTok for your current creator information every time the screen opens, and shows:

  • The nickname, @username and avatar of the TikTok account the video will be posted to.
  • A preview of the video and the caption, which you can edit (including hashtags).
  • Who can view this video: only the options TikTok returns for your account, with nothing selected by default. You cannot post until you choose one.
  • Allow comments, Duet and Stitch: all off by default. If you turned one off in your TikTok settings, it is greyed out.
  • Commercial content disclosure: off by default. Turned on, you choose “Your brand” (labeled “Promotional content”) and/or “Branded content” (labeled “Paid partnership”); you cannot post until you choose at least one. Branded content can only be visible to everyone or to friends, never “Only me”.
  • Your video's length against the maximum TikTok allows your account; a longer video cannot be posted.
  • The declaration, right before the button that sends the video: “By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation” (and the Branded Content Policy when it applies).

If TikTok says your account cannot post right now (for example, the daily limit), Melies stops and tells you TikTok's reason. Nothing is sent to TikTok until you confirm.

3. After you confirm

  • Melies uploads the exact video you rendered on your iPhone (no watermarks, no logos, no promotional content added) with the caption and settings you chose, using TikTok's Content Posting API (Direct Post, file upload).
  • We tell you that TikTok may take a few minutes to process the video before it appears on your profile, and we check its status with TikTok until it is published or fails. The result, with the link when TikTok gives one, appears in the app's Agenda › History.
  • A scheduled video waits on our server only until it is posted, and is deleted right after (48 hours at most). See the Privacy Policy.
